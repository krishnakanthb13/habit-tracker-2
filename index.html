💡 Complete User Manual

How to Use DailyHabits Pro

Build unstoppable routines, understand your consistency, protect hard-earned streaks, and master all unlocked Pro features with zero friction.

Quick Start in 4 Steps

Everything you need to know to build your daily momentum in under 60 seconds.

1

Create a Habit

Click + New Habit (or press N). Give it a name, select a color & category, and pick a tracking frequency (Daily, Specific Weekdays, or Numeric target).

2

One-Click Daily Check

Click any day cell to check it off. Enjoy satisfying tactile audio feedback and watch your achieved pill counter turn vibrant emerald green when you reach your goal.

3

Freeze Streaks on Rest Days

Sick, resting, or traveling? Alt+Click any cell to activate a Streak Freeze (🛡️). It preserves your streak without breaking momentum.

4

Reflect & Review

Right-click any cell to instantly record a daily reflection. Blue dots appear on the grid wherever a note exists so you can trace your thoughts across time.

Mastering Your Features

Explore the signature spreadsheet grid and unlocked tools.

The Signature Habit Spreadsheet

A high-density monthly table designed for speed. The habit column stays pinned as you scroll horizontally. Today's date is highlighted with an accent ring, and weekends are shaded for quick visual orientation.

🛡️

Streak Freeze (Skip Days)

In traditional habit trackers, a single missed day ruins your streak, causing psychological surrender (the What-The-Hell effect). Alt+Click any cell to place a freeze shield and keep your habit identity intact.

Numeric & Metric Habits

Track quantities rather than binary checks: e.g., 2,500 ml of water, 25 pages of reading, or 10,000 steps. Clicking a numeric cell lets you use quick stepper buttons to increment your logged amounts.

🧠 Science of Habits

Proven strategies from behavioral psychology.

🌱
Start Ridiculously Small

Make the barrier to entry tiny: read 2 pages or do 5 pushups. Consistency creates the identity; volume follows naturally.

🔗
Habit Stacking

Pair a new habit with an existing one: "After I pour my morning coffee [Current], I will meditate for 3 minutes [New]."

✌️
The Two-Day Rule

Never miss two days in a row. If life happens and you miss today, use Streak Freeze or prioritize completing just 1 minute tomorrow.
